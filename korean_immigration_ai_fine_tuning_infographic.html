1. Base Model Selection & Strategic Differentiation

Selecting the right foundation model determines the ultimate accuracy, reasoning capability, and resource requirement. While general-purpose frontier models (such as GPT-4 or Claude) require ongoing subscription fees and lack specialized hyper-focus, a fine-tuned 7B model provides high domain accuracy with zero API overhead.

Why Qwen 2.5 7B Instruct?

Qwen 2.5 7B exhibits exceptional instruction-following accuracy, strong structured output compliance (JSON/Markdown), and superior logical deduction over regulations without token fragmentation issues common in unoptimized multi-lingual small models.

  • ✓ Zero API Dependence: Complete local ownership and private execution capability.
  • ✓ English Strategy Alignment: Avoids tokenizer bloat while serving global expat populations in Korea.
  • ✓ QLoRA Compatibility: Full trainable adaptation achievable using less than 10GB VRAM.

2. Dataset Engineering & Data Pipeline

High-quality domain specialization depends on data quality rather than raw volume. Data is synthesized from official HiKorea guidelines, community visa experience logs (e.g., Facebook community threads, forums), and structured eligibility metrics into JSONL instruction-output pairs.

Dataset Composition by Visa Domain

The primary focus is allocated to F-2-7 point calculations and F-5 residency transitions.

JSONL Schema & Data Structuring

Raw unstructured text from policy documents and expat discussions is transformed into direct Q&A instructions containing concise rules and mandatory regulatory disclaimers.

// Example JSONL Instruction-Response Pair
{
"instruction": "What are the core requirements to apply for the F-2-7 points-based visa in South Korea?",
"output": "The F-2-7 visa requires evaluating criteria such as age, education level, annual income (GNI ratio), and Korean language proficiency (TOPIK/KIIP). Applicants must achieve a minimum score of 80 out of 175 total points. *Note: Always verify current GNI figures on HiKorea.*"
}
Instruction Curation Cleans informal forum queries into standard English questions covering eligibility, income, and legal stay notifications.
Output Verification Validates output facts against official immigration announcements to ensure absolute baseline accuracy.

3. Zero-Cost Fine-Tuning & Hardware Execution

By combining Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation (QLoRA) with modern acceleration libraries such as Unsloth, full fine-tuning of a 7B model can be completed entirely on standard consumer hardware without paid cloud GPU infrastructure.

Execution Steps & Adapter Merging

1

Quantization & QLoRA Setup

Load Qwen 2.5 7B in 4-bit precision to fit within laptop/desktop VRAM (<10 GB VRAM requirement).

2

Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)

Train LoRA adapter weights using Hugging Face TRL / Unsloth on the JSONL Q&A dataset.

3

Adapter Weight Merging

Merge learned LoRA adapters back into base 16-bit model weights for clean export and quick execution.
